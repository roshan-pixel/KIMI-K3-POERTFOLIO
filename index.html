We are a design studio passionate about unique, impactful, and timeless design. Where ambition wears elegance.

Led by Himanshi Parihar, Heavy is an independent brand design consultancy focused on strategy, visual identities, packaging, and tactile creative systems. Creating brands with heft since 2015 for clients all around the globe.

“Lead with empathy, design with intention, and never let a pixel exist without purpose.”

— Himanshi Parihar, Lead Designer & Creative Director

Core Capabilities

Brand Architecture

Positioning, brand verbal identity, tone of voice, narrative frameworks, and market differentiation.

Visual Identity

Logotypes, dynamic emblem systems, custom typography, color architectures, and comprehensive brand guidelines.

Packaging & Print

Bespoke structural packaging, die lines, foil stamping, sustainable substrates, and retail product presentation.

Digital & Creative Tech

Interactive portfolio experiences, modern web design systems, micro-animations, and AI-augmented creative workflows.
