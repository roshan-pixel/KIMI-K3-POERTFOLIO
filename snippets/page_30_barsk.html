What a time to be a dog. Barsk is a pet supplement brand based out of Singapore, born out of love for pets. For this brand, we created a full visual identity including logo, color palette, illustrations and packaging pieces. The idea was to contrast the idea of something pharmaceutical grade with a very colorful and energetic visual system that plays off of the idea of 70's style thumbnail illustrations.
