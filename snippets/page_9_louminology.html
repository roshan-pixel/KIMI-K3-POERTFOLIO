Louminology, established in 2022 by Leah Hollender in New York, stands out as a skincare brand that goes beyond the conventional. For Louminology, skincare is an act of self-care. Its mission is clear and impactful: to empower consumers to showcase their inner light with clinically proven products backed by rigorous scientific research.


The brand offers skincare solutions that highlight the inherent natural beauty of each individual through products that are both natural and effective, as well as luxurious and sustainable. The formulation of their products is based on high-quality Swiss ingredients, ethically sourced and backed by science, providing immediate results and long-term protection against everyday skin concerns, with an emphasis on brightening, anti-inflammation, and anti-aging prevention.
