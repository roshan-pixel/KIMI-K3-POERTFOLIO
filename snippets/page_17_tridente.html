In a time long forgotten, there were three navigators who explored distant lands in search of the perfect bean — a source of joy and vitality, the missing link to the human soul.


After journeying across the world, they gathered three beans and brought them to their sanctuary: a rocky formation by the sea.


The sanctuary was a magical place, a garden of Eden beneath the sun, where the beans were transformed into an enchanting elixir from which emanated aromas and flavors so intense and alluring that, like a siren’s song, they begged to be tasted.


Legend has it that when they drank it, they became three lush plants that to this day produce that magical elixir where the sea and the land meet: coffee in a cup.
