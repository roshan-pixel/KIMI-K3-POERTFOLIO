"Modern hot mexico" this is the core concept upon which we built the brand; references to Mexican visual culture used in a contemporary way. Digital illustrations that serve as a story and texture, paired with Mexican inspired typefaces and clean compositions. The aim is to create something authentically Mexican that comes off as modern, fun and non-cliché
