Winn Winn is a to-go-style cafe with a modified coffee menu, tailored pastry offerings, salads, and sandwiches. Co-founders and Columbus, Ohio natives, Audrey Plant and Natalie DiSabato Rothschild share a mutual passion for creating food that is approachable yet polished. Winn Winn’s menu comes from a nostalgic point of view of the founder’s childhood favorites with high-quality ingredients.


With this nostalgia in mind, we created visuals that reflect that comfort through illustrated patterns and classic typographic arrangements, translating the idea of a french bakery to americana cuisine. With amazing food and a brand to reflect it, we can say that this project was a win win.
